Short Bio

I am currently pursuing a Master's degree in Computer Technology at Wuhan University, where I am advised by Prof. Bo Du. I received my B.S. degree in Computer Science and Technology (Hongyi Program) from Wuhan University in 2025.

My current research can be broadly summarized as work on multi-agent systems under knowledge shift. More specifically, I am interested in how intelligent agents collaborate, adapt, and remain robust when knowledge, data distributions, or task environments are misaligned or evolving.
