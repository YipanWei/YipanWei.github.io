Short Bio

I am currently pursuing a Master's degree in Computer Technology at Wuhan University, where I am advised by Prof. Bo Du. I am also a research intern at Alibaba DAMO Academy. I received my B.S. degree in Computer Science and Technology (Hongyi Program) from Wuhan University in 2025.

I am interested in medical multimodal large models, federated learning, and multi-agent systems. Overall, my research focuses on vertical applications of Medical AI, including generalization, robustness, and trustworthiness.
